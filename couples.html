2013 · The beginning

We started shooting weddings.

Steph and I didn't have a business plan. We had a camera, a laptop, and a couple who said yes. Twelve years later we've delivered over 350 films. We know what your first dance looks like from behind the monitor. We know the toast your dad almost couldn't finish. We were there.

2018 · The pattern

Then the texts started.

Couples we filmed would reach out a year or two later. Not about video. About houses. "You and Steph bought that duplex, right? We're looking but don't know where to start." It kept happening. The same couples. The same question. They already trusted us.

The realization

We already knew their story.

Think about it. We'd spent 12 hours on the most important day of their lives. We knew their families, their priorities, how they made decisions together. Most agents meet you cold. We'd already been in the room.

2021 · Tonehomes

So we opened the second door.

We paid off $150K in debt. Bought our first duplex. House-hacked our way to six units. Learned the long game by living it. Now we help the couples we filmed do the same thing. First home, first investment, first "what's next." Same trust. New door.

Today · Full circle

Wedding film to front door.

Your listing film? I shoot that myself. Steph stages the room and redraws the floor plan. The couple who trusted us with their wedding day now trusts us with the biggest purchase of their lives. That part still gets me.
