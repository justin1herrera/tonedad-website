Plain-language privacy

What we collect,
and why.

When you browse tone.dad or contact Tonehomes, we collect only the information needed to understand the inquiry, respond to it, and see which parts of the site are useful.

Information you provide

An inquiry may include your name, email, optional phone number, service path, timeline, property details, budget, financing stage, renovation scope, and anything else you choose to share.

Website and attribution information

The site may record the page you landed on, referral source, campaign parameters, the call to action you used, and a temporary lead identifier. Vercel Analytics provides aggregated site-usage measurement.

How it is used

  • To respond to your inquiry and understand the decision in front of you.
  • To maintain a record of the inquiry and its source.
  • To measure which pages and calls to action are useful.
  • To protect the forms from spam and automated abuse.

Services that help operate the site

Information may be processed by the providers used to host the site, measure traffic, manage inquiry records, and send email notifications. Those providers currently include Vercel, Beehiiv, and Resend.

Your choices

You can ask what information is associated with your inquiry, request a correction, or ask that it be deleted. Text Justin at (609) 902-0299.
