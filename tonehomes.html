How we got here

It started with getting out of debt, not getting into a house.

We're not from money and nobody handed us a portfolio. Here's how it actually went.

2016

$150,000 of the kind of debt that takes things from you.

Student loans, a car, and vet bills for a dog we would have paid anything for. It took us three years and it was not glamorous.

"We ran an envelope system. Actual cash, split by category, and when an envelope was empty we were done until the next month."Notes 002 · Steph was right

Then we made one decision, and everything after it is downstream.

We weren't going back. The next dollar we borrowed had to pay us back — so we stopped borrowing for things that lose value and started borrowing for things that don't. The first one was a duplex. We lived in one side and rented the other.

What we'd tell you before you hire anybody

Including us. These are the five things we wish somebody had said out loud before our first one.

  1. 01

    Look at the space, not the listing.

    Ceiling height, a way in and out, heat, and where a bathroom could go. Nobody photographs those four.

  2. 02

    Price the fixer at the boring stuff.

    The invisible work is the budget. Treat the kitchen as the reward.

  3. 03

    Know who is designing it.

    Not the contractor. Somebody has to hold the finished room in their head for two months.

  4. 04

    Run the number first.

    Before you fall in love with the kitchen.

  5. 05

    Cosmetic has a budget.

    Structural has a hole in the middle of it, and nobody ever says the hole was smaller than they thought.
