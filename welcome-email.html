FILMING YOUR OWN PEOPLE THREE RULES
 
I've cut for Taylor, Katy, the Jets, NBC. I've shot 350 weddings. And the footage I actually care about is on my phone — three kids, my kitchen, terrible lighting.
Most people film their life wrong. Not their fault. Nobody tells you.
01
Keep rolling after it's over.
You stop the second the moment ends. The good part is always the three seconds after — the laugh that comes late, the kid looking back at you. Count to ten before you put the phone down. That's the whole trick.
02
Shoot hands.
Everybody shoots faces. You'll have a thousand photos of the face. What you won't have is how small their hands were, or the way your dad holds a coffee cup. Took me way too long to figure that out.
03
Say their name out loud.
While you're filming. Feels stupid. Do it anyway. Half of memory is sound, and one day that voice is going to matter more than the picture.
